## The Room

The desk lives in my living room, so it needs to feel at home here. I placed it by the windows for the natural light and kept the rest of the setup simple. The view definitely helps.

## The Desk

I switched from light oak to a Vernal hardwood standing desk in walnut. The darker wood took some getting used to, but I really like the warmth it brings to the room. At nearly 30 inches deep, it also gives me more space to spread out.

Vernal sponsored the video. I’m also using their matching monitor shelf, drawer, and cable-management rack. The laptops sit under the shelf, the smaller tech goes in the felt-lined drawer, and a large power strip underneath keeps things down to one cable running to the wall.

## The Chair

Still using the Herman Miller Aeron. I got it when a previous office was moving out, and it’s been one of the best additions to the setup. It feels a little office-y at home, but the adjustability and lumbar support make it worth keeping.

## Computers and Display

An M1 Pro MacBook Pro handles personal projects and video editing, while an M3 Pro is my work machine. Both live under the monitor shelf, so switching between them is a single cable swap. An external SSD gives me extra room for footage.

I’ve tried dual and vertical monitors, but I keep coming back to one good display. The Apple Studio Display gives me plenty of space at 5K, and its brightness holds up well in front of these windows. I use the built-in webcam, too.

## Lighting

The BenQ ScreenBar Halo keeps the desk lit without taking up surface space. I use the dial to warm things up in the evenings. This is probably when the setup feels its best.

## Peripherals

I’ve moved from mechanical keyboards to the Magic Keyboard with Touch ID. It’s quiet, wireless, and simple, and I like having Touch ID right there.

The Logitech MX Master has stayed. I’ve used it for years; this time I just changed the color. Underneath both is a basic felt desk mat. Nothing fancy, but it does the job.

## Audio

For recording, I use a Shure SM7dB on a low-profile Elgato arm, connected to a Behringer audio interface. The arm keeps the microphone easy to reach without sitting in the middle of the setup.

My older AirPods Max still work well for everyday listening. Otherwise, I use the Studio Display speakers, or AirPlay music to the Sonos Beam in the living room.

## Accessories

The Nomad Stand One keeps my phone charged and easy to grab. Turning it sideways gives me a little StandBy display, too.

I also keep an iPad mini below the monitor for music or a video. Universal Control lets me use the same mouse across both devices when I need that extra screen.

## Seating and Decor

A few plants, my camera, and the Silver Play Button give the space some personality. The lounge chair next to the desk gives me somewhere to take a break without staying in work mode.

That’s the setup for 2026. Enough room to code and create, while still feeling like home.

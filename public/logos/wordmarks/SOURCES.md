# Collaboration wordmarks

Original vector artwork is rendered directly, preserving white interior details, with monochrome colors and dark-mode inversion. Brand-specific dimensions balance visual weight. No lettering is recreated with the site's font.

Each brand is rendered as one complete source asset. Notion retains its original icon-and-wordmark lockup. Mobbin and Brilliant use their official standalone wordmarks; no custom icon-and-text combinations are assembled.

## Optical balancing

Notion and Mobbin use locally composed lockups from their original vector symbols and lettering, with independently tuned scale and spacing. These are layout adaptations, not untouched official combined assets. Brilliant remains wordmark-only.
